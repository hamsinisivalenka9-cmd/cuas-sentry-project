READ-ONLY QUERY PROTECTIONS
-------------------------------
dashboard/sql_validator.py::validate_read_only_sql() is defense-in-
depth on top of (not a substitute for) the mode=ro + PRAGMA query_only
connection every page already uses. It: strips line (--) and block
(/* */) comments before any other check, specifically so a keyword or
statement separator hidden in a comment cannot bypass validation;
rejects multiple statements by counting non-empty segments after
splitting on ';'; requires the (comment-stripped) statement to begin
with SELECT, WITH, EXPLAIN, or PRAGMA; and then searches the entire
statement -- not just its first word -- for any of 10 forbidden
keywords (INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, DROP, ALTER, CREATE, REPLACE, ATTACH,
DETACH, VACUUM) as a whole word, anywhere in the text. Documented
trade-off: a forbidden keyword is rejected even inside what could be a
string literal (e.g. SELECT 'DROP' AS label is rejected) -- this
validator favors over-rejection over risking a missed bypass. 18 unit
tests in tests/test_sql_validator.py cover valid statements, all 10
forbidden keywords, multi-statement rejection (plain and comment-
obscured), and comment-hidden-but-harmless forbidden text.
